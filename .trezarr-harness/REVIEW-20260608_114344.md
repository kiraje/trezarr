## Design

- Re-wrap = `opener + text + closer` on the joined text (opener at very start, closer at very end; internal `\n` preserved). New `SubLine` copies index/start_tc/end_tc verbatim, `raw=None`; new `SubDoc` carries metadata forward (D-92).
- **Wiring:** Step 8.6 (`engine.py:2043`) after the Pass-4 splice, before the Step-9 gate; the gate validates the wrapped text. Config `enable_envelope_preservation: bool = True`; False → early-return no-op. The iab H4 prompt rule stays as the first line of defense; this is the deterministic backstop.
