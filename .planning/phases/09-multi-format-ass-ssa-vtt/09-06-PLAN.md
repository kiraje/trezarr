<objective>
Wave 4 visual UAT: A human verifies that positioned ASS signs and VTT positioned cues render in their original screen position after translation. This is the final acceptance gate for Phase 9 success criterion 3: "signs render in their original screen position in a media player."

Purpose: Unit tests verify byte-identity (the structural bytes are preserved). But byte-identity alone cannot prove that a player correctly renders the position — a byte-level corruption could exist without triggering the round-trip hash check. A real player test is the only way to confirm that positioning tags ({\pos(x,y)}, {\an8}, VTT position: N%, line: N%) survive the translation pipeline intact and are interpreted correctly by a media player.

Output: No code produced. SUMMARY.md records the UAT outcome (pass/fail), the player used, and any issues found.
</objective>
