# Phase quick-260608-t53 Plan 01: CJK Credit-Line Check 9 Exemption Summary

**One-liner:** Narrow is_credit_fansub_cue() dual-condition exemption (keyword + short CJK ≤ 8) prevents fansub handle '虫二' from quarantining whole files via Check 9.

## What Was Built

**Problem:** Job #6 quarantined a 437-cue Moon Knight file on cue 437 because the model correctly rendered the Chinese credit prefix into Vietnamese (`Phụ đề dịch bởi:`) and left the fansub handle `虫二` (a proper-name signature, untranslatable) in place. Check 9's zero-false-positive CJK policy treated ANY CJK codepoint as an untranslated leak.

**Solution:** Added a narrow credit-line detector with two required conditions:
1. `CREDIT_FANSUB_RE` keyword present in source OR translated text
2. Total CJK/Hangul/Kana codepoint count in translated text ≤ `CJK_CREDIT_EXEMPT_MAX_CJK` (8)

Both must hold — neither alone fires the exemption. A fully-untranslated credit body (>8 CJK chars) still quarantines; a non-credit cue with a short CJK fragment still quarantines.

## Final Round: CJK Source-Marker Self-Exempt Class (260608-t53 round 4)

**Problem:** `CREDIT_FANSUB_RE` was matched against BOTH `src_text` and `trn_text`. Because the
translated output is Vietnamese, any CJK token in `trn_text` is the leak itself — not a credit
keyword. A model that leaked e.g. `翻译` into the translation would self-satisfy the keyword
condition (2 CJK chars ≤ 8 threshold) and wrongly escape quarantine even when the source had no
credit keyword.

**Fix:** Split into two regexes with distinct scopes:

- `CREDIT_SRC_RE` (matched against `src_text`): English "... by" attribution forms + Vietnamese
  credit prefixes + ALL Chinese fansub source markers (字幕組, 字幕组, 字幕, 翻譯, 翻译, 校對,
  校对, 時間軸, 时间轴, 壓制, 压制, 后期). Bare `字幕` restored here — it is safe on the source
  side (source text is not Vietnamese output).
- `CREDIT_TRN_RE` (matched against `trn_text`): Vietnamese credit prefixes + English "... by"
  forms ONLY. Zero CJK markers — any CJK in a Vietnamese output is always the leak to be counted,
  never a keyword.

`is_credit_fansub_cue()` signature unchanged. Logic: keyword present iff
`CREDIT_SRC_RE.search(src_text) OR CREDIT_TRN_RE.search(trn_text)`.
